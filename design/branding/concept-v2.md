## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Redesign the attached Gulu AI company logo into a new, much more Google-inspired identity: cheerful primary colors, clean flat simplicity, friendly geometric sans serif typography and generous white space. The input is the previous design and brand-name reference. Create ONE finished horizontal lockup centered on a pure white background, not a comparison sheet. Preserve the exact company name 'Gulu AI' but replace the heavy black type with lighter, bespoke, rounded geometric lettering at medium weight. Color the wordmark letter by letter: G bright blue, first u warm red, l sunny yellow, second u fresh green; the separated AI in blue. On the left use a distinctive open rounded G emblem with a subtle forward-moving terminal inspired by the original mark; simplify its geometry and divide its substantial stroke into harmonious blue, red, yellow and green solid color segments. Maintain an original Gulu silhouette and balanced negative space, rather than reproducing Google's exact G symbol. The overall feeling should be as approachable and effortless as a classic Google wordmark, polished for a serious AI productivity company. The emblem is only slightly taller than the wordmark, the spacing thoughtful, the composition calm and crisp. Flat vector-like artwork with perfectly solid fills and clean smooth edges. Wide landscape canvas, lockup occupying around 70 percent of width, ample white margins. No gradients, shadows, textures, sparkles, robots, brain motifs, tile background, 3D, slogans, captions or other text. Render Gulu AI exactly.
